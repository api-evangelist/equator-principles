The Equator Principles, in collaboration with the United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative (UNEP FI), has released a new publication to support Equator Principles Financial Institutions (EPFIs) in identifying, assessing and managing nature‑based risks in project-related finance, and also across broader financial activities. Nature loss is increasingly recognised as a source of material financial risk.
